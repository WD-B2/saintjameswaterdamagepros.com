How Saint James Water Damage Pros handles information.

Call (934) 223-1875

This policy explains what information we collect when you visit saintjameswaterdamagepros.com or call (934) 223-1875. Effective November 9, 2026.

Information we collect

When you call, we may collect your name, address, phone number and a description of the water damage so we can dispatch a crew. If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your request, dispatch crews, improve service and meet legal obligations. We may share job details with the restoration crew assigned to your address.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use forms. It may use basic analytics and embedded Google Maps, which can set their own cookies under their own policies.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete information we hold by emailing contact@saintjameswaterdamagepros.com. We do not sell personal information.
