Water damage restoration in Smithtown, NY is the job of getting water out of a building, drying the structure until meters read normal and rebuilding what could not be saved. Here the hard cases often come from the ground rather than the plumbing.

Our office in Saint James is about 3 miles from Smithtown, and we dispatch licensed and insured restoration professionals around the clock. This page starts with flood and drainage, then covers permits, nearby villages and the roads crews use.

Flood and drainage in Smithtown start with the Nissequogue River corridor

The Suffolk County Hazard Mitigation Plan ties flooding in the Town of Smithtown to the Nissequogue River and describes groundwater flooding along its Northeast Branch. It also notes that FEMA flood hazard maps do not address properties hit by groundwater. So a basement can flood without sitting in any mapped zone, and asking only if you are in a flood zone gives you the wrong answer.

A better question is where water sits after a long rain. Mills Pond and Millers Pond are both named in the Town's flood-prevention assessments, which tells you the ground near them holds water. If your floor-wall joint weeps after several wet days, the cause is probably saturated soil and not the roof.

What a high water table means for a wet Smithtown basement

When the water table climbs, groundwater pushes up through the slab, the cove joint and any floor drain. A sump pump may run all night, and if it fails or the power drops, the pit overflows. The result is often Category 2 water, which can carry soil and bacteria, so crews treat it differently from a clean supply leak.

Pumping a basement too fast while the yard is still saturated can stress the walls, so extraction is often done in stages while crews watch the readings. Our guide to basement water damage cleanup explains pad removal, drying and what to expect. If water is also coming across the yard, read about flood damage cleanup as well.

When water is spreading in Smithtown

Water that is still rising needs a different response from a pipe that has been shut. Note where it enters, how deep it is and whether the sump pump is running, then call the 24/7 line. A dispatcher will ask about the electrical panel, the heater and any floor drains before saying anything else.

Do not wade into a flooded basement to reach a breaker. PSEG Long Island advises against entering a room where water reached outlets, appliances or cords, and says it can shut power off at the meter if you cannot reach the breaker dry.

Before we arrive at your Smithtown home

A high water table or a heavy storm means you may have limited time to protect belongings. Use these steps in this order, skipping any that put you in water.

1

Check the sump pump and its discharge

If the pump is running, make sure the outlet line is not frozen, crushed or dumping next to the foundation. Do not unplug it while standing in water, and tell the dispatcher if it has stopped.

2

Shut the main only for a pipe problem

A groundwater leak will not stop at the main valve, but a failed supply line will. Close the main if you see spraying, hear running water in an empty house or find a wet wall.

3

Stay out of water that touched electrics

If it covers outlets or the furnace, treat it as live. Leave the room, and ask PSEG Long Island to cut power at the meter if you cannot reach the breaker dry.

4

Carry valuables up a level

Move boxes, documents and instruments from the floor to the first story. Basement shelves hold more than people think, and cardboard dies fast in damp air.

5

Wait on the tap water and the septic tank

After a heavy storm, Suffolk's Office of Emergency Management advises avoiding tap water until you are sure it is safe. If floodwater covers a septic tank, state health guidance says not to have the tank pumped.

Which office issues your permit, the Town or a nearby village

Smithtown is a hamlet and the seat of the Town of Smithtown, not an incorporated village, so the Town of Smithtown Building Department is the permit authority. Town Hall is on Main Street. Before you swap wiring, plumbing or framing, put the permit question to the Town of Smithtown Building Department.

Three incorporated villages sit nearby, the Village of the Branch, the Village of Nissequogue and the Village of Head of the Harbor. Each has its own government, so if your mailing address says Smithtown but you live in one of them, ask which office handles permits. A call to the wrong office wastes time you do not have.

What the Census numbers mean for your repair decision

The 2020 Census counted 25,629 people in the Smithtown CDP, and QuickFacts shows an owner-occupied rate of 88.6% and a median home value of $704,700. Most of the people reading this page own the wall that is wet. At those values, finished basements, built-ins and flooring can cost more to replace than the drying itself.

Ask for a written scope that separates drying from rebuild, so you can decide what to keep. Solid wood is the clearest example, and our page on hardwood floor water damage repair explains when boards can be dried in place and when they cannot.

Reaching you by NY 25, NY 25A, NY 347 and NY 454

Tell the dispatcher your cross street and one landmark. Many callers use the Smithtown Bull statue at the fork of Jericho Turnpike (NY 25) and St. Johnland Road (NY 25A). Others use Main Street near Town Hall, the Smithtown Bypass (NY 347), Veterans Memorial Highway (NY 454) or Bread and Cheese Hollow Road.

Crews start from Saint James, about 3 miles away by straight line. We do not promise an arrival time because storm traffic on these routes shifts quickly. Same-day response is offered when available, and the dispatcher will give you steps for the meantime.

Neighboring towns we serve

Frequently asked questions

Is emergency water removal available in Smithtown?

Yes. The dispatch line is answered 24/7, including storms and holidays. We offer same-day response when available and do not promise an arrival time, but the dispatcher will tell you how to protect the home until the crew gets there.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Smithtown?

We serve ZIP 11787, the Smithtown ZIP, which includes addresses around Main Street, Jericho Turnpike (NY 25), St. Johnland Road (NY 25A) and the NY 347 and NY 454 corridors. If your address is in one of the nearby incorporated villages, give the dispatcher your street so we can confirm.

Is my Smithtown home in a flood zone?

Very few Town properties lie in a FEMA special flood hazard area, but the Town had 220 NFIP policies as of July 13, 2020. Flood maps also do not capture groundwater flooding. Check the FEMA map for your address and ask your insurer about flood coverage, which typically has a 30-day waiting period.

Which permit office do I ask in Smithtown?

For most addresses it is the Town of Smithtown Building Department. The Village of the Branch, the Village of Nissequogue and the Village of Head of the Harbor have their own governments, so ask which office handles permits for your address.

Does homeowners insurance cover groundwater in my basement?

Flood losses are generally not provided in standard homeowners or tenants policies, according to New York's Department of Financial Services. Sudden and accidental plumbing leaks are often covered, and gradual seepage often is not. Read your policy, ask your insurer and document the damage before cleaning up.

What should I do if my sump pump stops during a storm?

Call the dispatch line, turn off power to the area if you can do it from a dry spot and move belongings off the floor. A battery backup or a spare pump helps next time, and you can ask a licensed plumber about options.

How long will it take to dry a flooded basement?

Often several days. Wet concrete and framing release moisture slowly, and the crew reads moisture meters on repeat visits until the readings reach the dry standard. Ground saturation can stretch the timeline.
