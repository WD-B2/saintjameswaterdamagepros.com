What we will not do

We do not promise arrival times, quote prices before a scope exists or tell you a claim will be paid. Insurance decisions belong to your insurer. We document the damage so the conversation is easier.

What happens after you call

The dispatcher asks where the water started, which rooms are wet and whether anyone has smelled gas or seen sparks. Those answers decide which crew is sent and what equipment goes on the truck. A burst supply line needs pumps and air movers, while a sewage backup needs protective gear and disinfectants.

You hear the plan in plain language before work starts. That covers what will be removed, what will be dried in place and what is left for a separate rebuild. Questions are welcome at every step, and you are never pushed into a scope you do not understand.

The equipment crews use

Restoration is measured work. Crews carry moisture meters to read wet wood and drywall, thermal imaging cameras to find water behind walls and extraction units to pull standing water from floors and carpet. Drying uses air movers and LGR or desiccant dehumidifiers, with daily readings to confirm progress.

Industry standards such as ANSI/IICRC S500 describe good practice for water damage and S520 covers mold. We encourage you to ask any company, including us, which published standards its crews follow.

Why local matters in Saint James

Saint James sits in the Town of Smithtown in Suffolk County, and the permit office for most buildings here is the Town of Smithtown Building Department. Nearby villages such as Lake Grove and Islandia run their own building departments, which affects where a repair permit question goes.

Water also behaves differently in different houses. Basements, crawl spaces and slab foundations each fail in their own way. Our basement cleanup and crawl space pages explain the differences.

Working with your insurance

Insurance decisions belong to your insurer, so we do not predict outcomes. What we can do is record moisture readings, photograph the damage and describe the work in clear terms. That paperwork makes a claim conversation easier for most homeowners.

Sudden and accidental losses, such as a burst pipe, are often covered. Gradual leaks and flooding often are not. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and call us when water is still spreading.

Services we dispatch for

The crews we dispatch handle the full range of residential water losses in Saint James, NY. Pipe failures include burst pipes, frozen pipes and slab leaks. Appliance and HVAC losses include appliance leaks and AC condensate leaks.

Cleanup work covers sewage backups, structural drying and the repair of wet floors and walls. Each of those pages explains the equipment, the likely scope and the questions worth asking before you agree to anything.

Questions worth asking any restoration company

Ask what the scope includes and what it leaves out. Ask whether drying readings will be recorded and shared. Ask who handles rebuild work after the structure is dry, and whether that is priced separately.

A clear answer to those questions tells you more than any slogan. We answer them on the first call, and we put the plan in plain words before work begins.
